# COMPLAINT FOR BREACH OF LEASE (excerpt)

1. Defendant Priya Raman leased Unit 3B, 1180 Alder Street, from Plaintiff Ridgeline Property Management LLC from July 1, 2024 to June 30, 2026.
2. Defendant vacated on April 15, 2026 without notice and failed to pay rent for April, May and June 2026 ($2,950 per month, $8,850 total).

4. Plaintiff demands $14,380 plus costs and attorney fees. Defendant's security deposit of $2,950 was applied to cleaning.
