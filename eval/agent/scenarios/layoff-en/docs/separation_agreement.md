# Separation Agreement and General Release (excerpt)

1. Severance. Halcyon Freight Systems will pay Employee $11,250 in equal installments over six weeks.
2. General release. Employee releases all claims, known or unknown, arising from employment, including claims under the Age Discrimination in Employment Act.
3. Non-disparagement. Employee will not make negative statements about the Company. The Company's obligations are not stated.
4. Non-solicitation. For 12 months Employee will not solicit Company customers or employees.
5. Review period. Employee has 21 days to consider this Agreement and 7 days after signing to revoke it.
